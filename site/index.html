Tier roles

  • Tiers 1 and 2 build a foundation that stays useful as a crosspath, including secondary attacks and conveniences, not just numeric boosts.
  • Tier 3 is the commitment point: only one path may pass tier 2.
  • Tier 4 pays off the branch; tier 5 extends it along the same axis rather than stacking the other branches.
  • Middle path tier 4 introduces a manual ability in 25 of 26 towers; tier 5 retains it.

Legality and economy

  • Every standard tower exposes exactly 63 purchasable crosspaths with zero build rule violations.
  • Final to previous tier ratios run 1.58 to 19.64, median 6.15 across 78 paths: single purchases, not output multipliers.
  • Heroes progress on XP (213560 to 365190 total), never on cash upgrades.
